# Client Intake and Referral Process

## Basic Flow

1. The client approaches the PACD (Public Assistance and Complaints Desk).
2. PACD collects the minimum information needed to identify the client, record the concern, and determine the appropriate service window.
3. PACD refers the client to the relevant program-in-charge or service window.
4. The assigned program-in-charge receives the referral and collects any additional information needed to handle the case.

## PACD First-Touch Intake

Record the following when relevant:

- Intake date and time
- Client name and a temporary queue or client number
- Preferred contact method and contact number, if follow-up is needed
- Whether the client is an OFW, a former OFW, or a family member; country of employment if relevant
- The service or assistance the client is seeking
- The client's concern in their own words (verbatim); note the language if useful
- Any immediate safety issue or urgent deadline
- Referred program or service window
- PACD staff member who recorded the intake

## Recording the Concern

Keep the client's verbatim statement separate from the staff's summary or assessment. Do not change the meaning of the client's words. Record only relevant details and avoid adding assumptions.

## Referral and Follow-Up

PACD directs the client to the appropriate window and records the referral. The receiving program-in-charge reviews the concern, confirms the client's needs, and collects further case details or documents only when necessary to provide the service.

## Privacy and Data Minimization

Explain why the information is being collected and which program or staff may receive it. Collect only information needed for intake, routing, follow-up, or an urgent response. Do not request sensitive documents or detailed personal information at first touch unless they are necessary for routing or immediate assistance.
